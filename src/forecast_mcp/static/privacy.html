Event Staffing Planner

Privacy

This research prototype stores only the data it needs to operate. It uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Data stored

  • Sign-in identity: the identifier issued by your sign-in provider, and your email address and name if the provider shares them.
  • Workspace activity: notes you enter or give to Claude (stored as written), the constraints derived from them, your scenarios and selected plans, and an activity log with the author and time of each change.
  • Technical logs: request logs (IP address, time, page) kept by the server and its hosting provider for secure operation.

Data not stored

  • Your Claude conversations. The server receives only the tool calls Claude makes, such as a note recorded on your behalf.
  • Passwords. Authentication is handled by the sign-in provider.

Access

Your workspace is visible only to you. The operators of this server can access the database to run and improve the service.

Deletion

"Reset workspace" in the account menu deletes all workspace data. To delete your account and identity record, contact the operators of this server.

The theme park, its entrances and its visitor history are fictional. Forecasts and plans are computed from this synthetic history for demonstration only and are not operational advice.
