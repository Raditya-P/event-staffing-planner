Event Staffing Planner

Privacy

This is a research prototype. It keeps only what it needs to work and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

What is stored

  • Your sign-in identity: the ID your sign-in provider gives us, plus your email address and name if it shares them.
  • What you do in your workspace: notes you type or tell Claude (kept word for word), the changes made from them, your what-ifs and chosen plans, and a history of who changed what and when.
  • Technical logs: request logs (address, time, page) kept by the server and its host for running it securely.

What is not stored

  • Your Claude conversations. The server only receives the tool calls Claude makes, such as a note it writes down for you.
  • Passwords. Signing in is handled by the sign-in provider.

Who can see it

Your workspace is visible only to you. The people running this server can access the database to operate and improve it.

Deleting it

"Start over with a fresh demo" in the account menu deletes everything in your workspace. To delete your account and identity record, contact the people running this server.

The theme park, its entrances and its visitor history are made up. Forecasts and plans are calculated from that invented history, as a demonstration, not as operational advice.
